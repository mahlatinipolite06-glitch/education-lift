Who We Are & What We Do

EduLift is a dedicated educational support platform created to bridge the funding gap for students who have completed Grade 12 or equivalent studies and wish to pursue higher education, college, or university programs.

1. Complete the Form

Tell us about your educational background, your chosen course, funding goals, and personal story.

2. Verification & Profile

We review your details and create a verified profile so compassionate donors can discover your goals.

3. Receive Funding

Donors sponsor your tuition and learning expenses directly, enabling you to focus on your education.

Who Can Apply?

Graduating Students

Students who have finished Grade 12 and need financial support to enroll in university or college.

Clear Academic Goals

Dedicated individuals with passion for their chosen field of study (e.g. Science, Nursing, Technology, Arts).

Demonstrated Need

Students who face genuine financial constraints that would otherwise prevent them from pursuing education.
