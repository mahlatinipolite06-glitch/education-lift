About EduLift

Many students complete Grade 12 but cannot continue to university or college because of financial difficulties. EduLift aims to bridge this gap by connecting students with donors who are willing to support their educational dreams.

How EduLift Works

1. Students Register

Students submit their educational information and explain their needs.

2. Donors Explore

Donors explore student profiles and learn about their goals.

3. Education Support

Donors can choose students they would like to support.
